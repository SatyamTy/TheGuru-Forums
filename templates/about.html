About This Website:

This is a online discussion site dedicated to Theguru school's community, where students, teachers or even parents can post questions, discussion topics and interract with other user.
This site is made in html, css and java script as front-end languages and the back end is made by django.
The website is still in it's early stages and users might encounter with some minor bugs.

About The Guru:

The Guru is a co-educational day boarding school. Affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education, New Delhi (CBSE), the school is a path-breaker in the pursuit of excellence. Life at this institution centers on a shared commitment to academic excellence, intellectual growth, cultural brilliance, arts, sportsmanship, high standards of ethical awareness & community service. The school motto "Nurturing Excellence" upholds the founders’ pledge to excellence in all fields.
